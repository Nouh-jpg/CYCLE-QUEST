# Cycle Quest - Design Document

## Overview
A high-quality, "Temple Run" style infinite runner where the player rides a bike through challenging environments.

## Target Platforms
- Google Play Store
- Steam
- Nintendo Switch

## Characters
- **Maya** (Girl): Energetic and adventurous.
- **Jax** (Boy): Bold and skilled.
- *Player can choose between the two.*

## Core Mechanics
- Infinite scrolling environment.
- Lane switching (Left, Center, Right).
- Jumping and Sliding.
- Bike-specific mechanics (e.g., drafting, boosts).

## Visual Style
- **Stylized/Low Poly**: Bright, clean, high-performance aesthetic (similar to Fortnite/Among Us). Optimized for Switch and Mobile.
